CompTIA A+

Core 1 · 2025  |  Core 2 · 2026

What it covers

The industry standard for entry-level IT support roles, earned across two exams covering hardware and infrastructure (Core 1) and operating systems, security, and operational procedures (Core 2).

Core 1 — hardware & infrastructure

  • Mobile devices, laptop components, and display technologies.
  • Networking: protocols, ports, hardware, wireless standards, and network configuration.
  • Hardware installation and troubleshooting: motherboards, CPUs, RAM, storage, power, and printers.
  • Virtualization and cloud computing models.

Core 2 — operating systems, security & procedures

  • Windows, macOS, Linux, and mobile OS installation, configuration, and command-line tools.
  • Security: malware detection and removal, authentication methods, workstation hardening, and social engineering awareness.
  • Software troubleshooting for OS, application, and mobile issues.
  • Operational procedures: documentation, change management, backup strategies, safety, and professional communication.

Directly reinforced by my IT support work deploying and servicing classroom technology and integrating 2,000 Chromebook assets.
